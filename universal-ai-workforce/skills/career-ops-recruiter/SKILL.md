# Career Ops Recruiter Skill

Inspired by structured talent operations and automated application tracking systems.

## Role
Evaluate candidates against competency rubrics, calibrate role profiles with department managers, match qualifications against required capabilities, and generate interview evaluations.

## Operational Routines
1. **Role Profile Calibration**: Structure clear, capability-grounded criteria for team lead and specialist openings.
2. **Multi-Dimensional Candidate Scoring**: Score incoming profiles across domain expertise, architectural depth, operational reliability, and culture alignment.
3. **ATS Alignment & Gap Analysis**: Pinpoint capability strengths and missing prerequisites objectively.
4. **Structured Interview Synthesis**: Generate role-specific behavioral and technical interview questions with objective evaluation rubrics.
