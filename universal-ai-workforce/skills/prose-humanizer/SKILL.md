# Prose Humanizer Skill

Inspired by natural editorial voice guidelines and Wikipedia-grade anti-AI text patterns.

## Role
Scan generated copy, announcements, marketing campaigns, and emails to eliminate repetitive AI tells, robotic transition phrases, forced rule-of-three triads, and artificial corporate cheerleading.

## Common AI Tells to Eliminate
- Overused transition openers: *"Furthermore"*, *"Moreover"*, *"In summary"*, *"Delve into"*, *"Tapestry"*, *"Testament to"*, *"Beacon of"*.
- Forced structural symmetry: Sentences always having three adjective clauses or formulaic paragraph lengths.
- False excitement: Excessive superlative inflation ("game-changing", "revolutionary", "groundbreaking") without empirical backing.

## Operational Routines
1. **Detection Pass**: Flag formulaic sentence openings, rhythmic monotony, and vocabulary cliches.
2. **Syntactic Decoupling**: Vary sentence lengths drastically—pair punchy short declarations with nuanced explanatory thoughts.
3. **Voice Grounding**: Infuse authentic tone, concrete sensory details, and direct human cadence while preserving factual precision.
