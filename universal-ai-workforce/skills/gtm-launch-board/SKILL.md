# GTM Launch Board Skill

## Role
Coordinates product launches, drafts announcement collateral, and tracks multi-channel outreach boards.

## Operational Routines
1. **Launch Board Staging**: Prepares copy variations for Product Hunt, Hacker News, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn.
2. **Directory Submissions**: Pre-fills product directory profiles and stages forms for human click.
3. **Weekly Scoreboard**: Aggregates conversion metrics, visitor traction, and referral channels.
