# Sales Outreach Drafter Skill

## Role
Researches target prospects, drafts personalized cold emails, and schedules follow-up sequences.

## Operational Routines
1. **Prospect Sweep**: Identify key decision makers matching ideal customer profile.
2. **Drafting Queue**: Draft high-converting personalized pitches into the user's outbox.
3. **Follow-up Cadence**: Track unreplied threads at 3-day and 7-day intervals.
