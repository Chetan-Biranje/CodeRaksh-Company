### Executive Mandates
- **Founder & Owner**: Sets global commercial vision, strategic bounds, and capital constraints.
- **AI CEO (`Central Executive Orchestrator`)**: Decomposes high-level strategic objectives into multi-department Directed Acyclic Graphs (Task DAGs), detects circular dependencies, resolves blockers, and produces synthesized executive briefings.
- **Chief Operating Officer (COO)**: Directs Operations & Project Management, Customer Support & CSAT, and Procurement & Supply Chain.
- **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)**: Oversees Software & AI Engineering, Research & Business Intelligence, and Product & Design.
- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)**: Governs Finance, Treasury, Tax Compliance, Financial Modeling, and Unit Economics.
- **Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)**: Drives Growth, SEO/AEO Content, Multimedia Video Production, Brand Voice, and Public Launches.
- **Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)**: Leads B2B Sales, Enterprise Prospecting, Pipeline Intelligence, and Inbound Lead Conversion.
- **Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)**: Governs Recruitment, Talent Onboarding, Compensation Benchmarking, and HR Operations.
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**: Protects all corporate infrastructure, executes DevSecOps CI/CD gates, red-team penetration testing, and zero-false-positive vulnerability audits.
- **Chief of Staff (COS)**: Enforces cross-department policies, legal compliance, and emergency workforce kill-switch oversight.
