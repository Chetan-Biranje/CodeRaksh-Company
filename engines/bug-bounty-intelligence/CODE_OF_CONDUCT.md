# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing
other people's private information are not acceptable.

Security research ethics apply on top: no testing of systems you are not authorized to test, no
sharing of confidential vulnerability details, and respect for each program's rules and
disclosure timelines.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or ban contributors who break these rules. Report
conduct concerns to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or a direct
message to a maintainer listed on the repository.
